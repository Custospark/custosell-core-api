## Technical reference (source of truth)

**Screens:** `/hr` [M] (employees, roles, payroll, attendance)

**User actions (FE hooks):** `useCreateEmployee` · `useUpdateEmployee` ·
`useRoles` · `useRolePermissions` · `usePayroll` ·
`useCreatePayrollPeriod` · `useAttendance` · `useEmployeeShiftLog`

**API endpoints (BE):** `/hr/employees` CRUD · `/hr/roles` +
`/hr/roles/{id}/permissions` · `/hr/payroll` CRUD +
`/hr/payroll/periods` · `/hr/attendance`
