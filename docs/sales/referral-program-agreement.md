# Custosell Referral & Sales Representative Program Agreement

**Prepared by:** Custospark Company Ltd

## Parties

This Agreement is made between:

- **Custospark Company Ltd** ("**Custospark**" or the "**Company**"), a company incorporated in
  Uganda with its principal place of business in Kampala, Uganda
  (www.custospark.com, info@custospark.com), the company behind the **Custosell** business
  operating system (www.custosell.com), on the one part; and

- the participant named in the signature block below ("**Participant**"), who may be an
  individual or a business entity, on the other part.

Custospark and the Participant are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

---

## 1. Definitions

- **Custosell** means the business operating system and related services operated by Custospark,
  including its point-of-sale, inventory, accounting, online storefront, and referral features.
- **Referral Program** means the program under which the Participant refers new businesses to
  Custosell and earns rewards.
- **Sales Representative Program** means the program under which the Participant is formally
  enrolled as a sales representative of Custosell and earns commissions.
- **Referred Business** means a new business that signs up for a Custosell subscription using the
  Participant's referral code or referral link.
- **Referral Code** means the unique code, link, or QR code generated for the Participant inside
  Custosell.
- **Subscription Plan** means the Custosell plan actually selected and paid for by a Referred
  Business (Essential, Professional, Enterprise, or Personal; monthly or yearly).
- **Net Payment** means the amount actually received by Custospark from a Referred Business for
  its subscription, after discounts, refunds, chargebacks, reversals, and any applicable taxes or
  fees.
- **Reward** means the referral reward earned by the Participant.
- **Commission** means the sales-representative commission earned by the Participant.
- **Earnings** means the Participant's combined Rewards and Commissions.
- **Payout** means the transfer of Earnings to the Participant.

---

## 2. Purpose

Custospark operates Custosell and wishes to reward the Participant for referring new businesses
to Custosell and, where the Participant is enrolled as a sales representative, for promoting and
selling Custosell. The Participant wishes to refer businesses to Custosell and, where enrolled,
to act as a sales representative. This Agreement sets out the terms on which the Participant
earns Rewards and Commissions and how Earnings are paid.

---

## 3. Enrollment & Eligibility

3.1 **Enrollment.** The Participant joins the Referral Program by creating an account on
Custosell and generating a Referral Code. The Participant joins the Sales Representative Program
by completing the separate enrollment steps provided by Custospark in-app, which includes
confirming their name or business name, contact details, and payout details.

3.2 **Eligibility.** The Participant must:
- be at least 18 years of age (individuals) or a duly registered entity (businesses);
- provide accurate and current contact and payout information;
- not be an employee, director, or agent of Custospark's direct competitors in the business
  management software sector;
- comply with all applicable laws.

3.3 **Custospark's discretion.** Custospark may decline an application for enrollment in its
reasonable discretion and may suspend or close an account that fails to meet the requirements of
this Agreement.

---

## 4. Referral Rewards

4.1 **Referral Code.** Custospark will generate one Referral Code for the Participant, which may
be shared as a copyable code, a signup link, or a QR code.

4.2 **Earning a Reward.** The Participant earns a Reward when all of the following occur:
- a new business signs up for a Custosell subscription using the Participant's Referral Code or
  link;
- that Referred Business completes registration and activates a Subscription Plan; and
- Custospark receives the first payment under that Subscription Plan.

4.3 **Reward amount.** The Reward is **[10]%** of the Net Payment actually received by Custospark
from the Referred Business under its Subscription Plan, unless Custospark communicates a
different rate. Rewards are calculated on what is actually paid, not on a plan's sticker price.

4.4 **One code per Referred Business.** Each Referred Business may be associated with only one
Referral Code. Where more than one referral source is claimed for the same business, the first
valid referral recorded by Custospark will apply, and Custospark's records are conclusive.

4.5 **No limit.** There is no limit to the number of Referred Businesses the Participant may
refer under this Agreement.

4.6 **Referred Business discount.** Where a Referred Business applies the Participant's Referral
Code at signup, it may receive a discount on its subscription. Such discount does not reduce the
basis on which the Participant's Reward is calculated where the Reward is calculated on Net
Payment; in all cases the Reward is based on the amount actually paid.

---

## 5. Sales Representative Commissions

5.1 **Enrollment.** The Participant may be enrolled in the Sales Representative Program, in which
case a Sales Representative badge and the Participant's commission rate appear on the
Participant's Referral Dashboard in Custosell.

5.2 **Commission rate.** The Participant's commission rate is **[10]%** per qualifying sale, of
the type shown in the Participant's Referral Dashboard (percentage of Net Payment), unless
Custospark communicates a different rate in writing.

5.3 **Earning a Commission.** A Commission is earned when:
- the Participant, while enrolled as a sales representative, is the recorded source of a new
  subscription; and
- Custospark receives the corresponding Net Payment.

5.4 **Combined Earnings.** Rewards and Commissions are combined into one Earnings balance shown
on the Participant's Referral Dashboard.

---

## 6. Earnings Calculation, Timing & Adjustments

6.1 **Timing.** Earnings accrue once Custospark has received the corresponding Net Payment.
Earnings are credited to the Participant's Referral Dashboard, which is the record of record.

6.2 **Refunds and reversals.** If a Referred Business cancels, is refunded, or reverses payment,
Custospark may deduct the corresponding Earnings (or credit such Earnings against future
Earnings) to the extent not yet paid out.

6.3 **Taxes and fees.** Net Payment is calculated after discounts, refunds, chargebacks,
reversals, and applicable taxes or fees. The Participant is responsible for any taxes payable on
Earnings, and Custospark may withhold amounts where required by law.

6.4 **No interest.** Earnings do not accrue interest.

---

## 7. Payment & Payouts

7.1 **Payout method.** Payouts are made by Mobile Money or bank transfer to the payout details the
Participant has set in Custosell (which may be to any bank worldwide). The Participant must ensure
these details are accurate.

7.2 **Payout threshold.** Payouts are processed once Earnings reach **[20 USD]** (or its
equivalent), or on Custospark's regular payout cycle, whichever Custospark applies.

7.3 **Failure of payout.** If a Payout cannot be completed because the Participant's details are
incorrect or the payout is rejected, Custospark will notify the Participant and will attempt the
Payout again once corrected details are provided.

7.4 **No cash or non-program use.** Earnings have no value outside this program, cannot be
transferred, and cannot be exchanged for anything other than Payouts under this Agreement.

---

## 8. Prohibited Conduct

The Participant must not, and must not cause or permit others to:

8.1 refer themselves, or any business in which the Participant (or any person acting on their
behalf) holds a material interest, in order to earn Earnings;

8.2 use a second account or referrer identity, or otherwise create duplicate or fraudulent
referrals;

8.3 generate false, misleading, or spam referrals, including unsolicited bulk messaging;

8.4 misrepresent Custosell, its features, pricing, or this program to any person;

8.5 make or publish any earnings, income, or success claims that are false or misleading;

8.6 use Custospark's or Custosell's trademarks, logos, or materials without authorization, or in
any way that implies an employment, agency, or partnership relationship;

8.7 engage in any conduct that damages Custospark's reputation or that violates applicable law.

Any Earnings arising from prohibited conduct are forfeited, and Custospark may terminate this
Agreement under Clause 12.

---

## 9. No Employment, Agency or Partnership

9.1 The Participant is an independent participant in Custospark's programs. This Agreement does
not create an employment relationship, a joint venture, a partnership, or an agency.

9.2 Neither Party is authorised to enter into contracts or incur obligations on behalf of the
other Party, and the Participant must not represent otherwise.

9.3 The Participant is not entitled to employee benefits, statutory deductions, or labour-law
protections as a result of this Agreement.

---

## 10. Confidentiality

The Participant will keep confidential any non-public information received from Custospark about
the program, pricing, plans, or customers ("**Confidential Information**") and will use it only
to perform this Agreement. This obligation does not apply to information that is or becomes
publicly available without the Participant's breach, or that the Participant is required to
disclose by law. This Clause survives termination of this Agreement.

---

## 11. Intellectual Property

11.1 All rights in Custosell, Custospark, their logos, trademarks, product designs, content, and
software remain with Custospark or its licensors.

11.2 The Participant may refer to Custosell as a product they promote, using materials provided
by Custospark, but may not register, imitate, or create derivative marks.

---

## 12. Term & Termination

12.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the date the Participant first generates a Referral Code
or enrolls in the Sales Representative Program and continues until terminated.

12.2 **Termination by the Participant.** The Participant may terminate at any time by closing
their referral participation in Custosell or by written notice to Custospark.

12.3 **Termination by Custospark.** Custospark may terminate this Agreement at any time by
written or in-app notice, and may terminate immediately upon any breach of this Agreement or any
prohibited conduct under Clause 8.

12.4 **Effect of termination.** Upon termination, the Participant's right to earn further
Earnings ends. Earned but unpaid Earnings for qualifying subscriptions completed before
termination will be paid out within **[60]** days of termination, except where the Participant
was terminated for breach of this Agreement, in which case such Earnings may be forfeited at
Custospark's discretion.

13.1 Custospark provides the programs and Custosell on an "as is" and "as available" basis and,
to the maximum extent permitted by law, excludes all warranties, whether express or implied.

13.2 To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental,
special, or consequential damages, or for loss of profits or revenue, arising out of this
Agreement. Custospark's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the Earnings paid to
the Participant in the twelve months preceding the claim.

## 14. Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

14.1 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda.

14.2 **Negotiation.** The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith
negotiation.

14.3 **Mediation.** If negotiation fails, the Parties will attempt mediation through a mediator
mutually agreed in Kampala, Uganda.

14.4 **Jurisdiction.** Any dispute not resolved by negotiation or mediation may be referred to
the courts of Uganda, and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of
Kampala, Uganda.

---

## 15. General

15.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties
and supersedes any prior agreements or communications about the program.

15.2 **Amendments.** Custospark may amend this Agreement by posting a revised version in Custosell
or on custosell.com. Continued participation after the effective date of the amendment
constitutes acceptance.

15.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder remains in force.

15.4 **No waiver.** A failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of it.

15.5 **Assignment.** The Participant may not assign this Agreement without Custospark's prior
written consent.
