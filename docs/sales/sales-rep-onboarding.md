## 3. What Custosell Is

Custosell is an all-in-one business operating system: **Point of Sale (POS),
E-commerce Storefront, Inventory & Supply Chain, Accounting, HR & Payroll,
Invoicing, Expenses, Project Management, Sales Pipeline (CRM), Financial
Forecasting, and Document Management - all in one connected system that works
with or without the internet.**

**One-sentence pitch:**
> "Custosell is the one system that runs your whole business - selling, stock,
> books, staff, customers, and your online shop - in a single place that keeps
> working even when the internet goes down."

### 3.1 The Problem It Solves

Most small businesses juggle separate apps and paper: one tool for selling,
another for stock, spreadsheets for money, notebooks for staff, and WhatsApp
for customers. When the internet drops, the till stops. When it is tax time,
receipts are everywhere. Custosell replaces this mess with one connected
system where every sale updates stock, books, and reports automatically - and
where nothing stops when the network fails.

### 3.2 What Makes It Different

1. **Offline-first.** Sell, record payments, add products and customers, and
   run shifts even with no internet. Everything syncs automatically when the
   connection returns - no re-entering data.
2. **All-in-one.** POS, storefront, inventory, accounting, HR, CRM, projects,
   expenses, forecasting, and documents in one subscription. No juggling apps.
3. **Books that write themselves.** Every sale, refund, expense, invoice, and
   payment posts a balanced accounting entry automatically.
4. **One shop, online and offline.** Your physical till and your public online
   shop share the same stock, customers, and orders.
5. **Built for the whole business journey.** From a first shop with a few items
   to a multi-branch operation with staff and payroll.

## 6. Product Deep Dive - What Each Tool Does

### 6.1 Getting Started, Accounts & Security

A business can be live in about five minutes: register, verify your email,
create the business, complete onboarding, and land on the dashboard ready to
sell. Beyond business accounts there are **personal accounts** (for expenses,
budgets, notes) and **free shopping accounts** (to browse storefronts and buy
without any business setup). Multiple accounts can be linked under one login
and switched with one tap, with a default "Primary" account for billing and
referrals.

Security is built in: email verification, two-factor authentication (2FA),
code-confirmed password/email changes, a profile photo that follows linked
accounts, and an **Account Activity** feed showing every sign-in and security
change with device and IP details.

### 6.2 Sales & POS (the heart of the product)

Cashiers find products by name, SKU, or barcode - or scan a barcode for
instant add-to-cart. The cart supports quantity changes, retail vs wholesale
pricing per line, single-line discounts, and whole-cart discounts. Checkout
handles **cash (with automatic change calculation), Mobile Money, card, or
"other"**, partial payments/installments, and attaching a walk-in customer by
name. Every completed sale updates stock, books, and the dashboard
simultaneously and produces a receipt you can print, download as PDF, or
email.

Orders can be held ("John's order"), resumed, renamed, updated, or cancelled;
storefront/online orders can be handled with delivery and WhatsApp contact;
sales history is searchable; and refunds are clean (full or partial,
item-by-item, with limits guarded so you can never refund more than was paid).
Invoicing is built in - from a completed sale or the live cart - including
full B2B invoicing with send/track/paid status. The entire flow works offline
and auto-syncs.

### 6.3 Shifts

Shifts tie every sale to a person and a time window, so "who sold what" is
always traceable. Staff open a shift (clock in), record the opening cash
float, record drawer-paid expenses, balance the drawer mid-day, and close with
a full reconciliation (Balanced / Over / Short - mismatches are recorded, not
blocked). The **Shift Close Report** summarizes gross, expenses, refunds, VAT,
net sales, cash by method, and expected drawer. Live shift tracking, history,
and staff handovers all work offline.

### 6.4 Inventory, Products & Categories

Build a full catalog: products and service items, categories, retail/wholesale/
cost pricing, tax class and percentage, SKUs and barcodes, opening stock and
low-stock thresholds, sale discounts for the public shop, recurring/
subscription flags, and product photos. Products can be edited, deactivated
(history kept) or deleted; the catalog is searchable and filterable, with bulk
actions (list/unlist for shop and supply, delete).

Stock is adjusted up or down with a recorded reason and can never go below
zero. Every movement is logged - per-product stock history and the full
**Stock Ledger** give a complete audit trail. Stock can be transferred between
branches, a full catalog can be imported from Excel (with per-row error
reports) or exported, and an **Inventory Overview** shows stock value, profit,
alerts, and margin rankings.

### 6.5 Marketplace & Purchase Orders (B2B supply)

Restock from the Custosell marketplace: save regular suppliers to "My
suppliers", browse any supplier's catalog, build a purchase-order cart, and
save a draft or submit. Every order is tracked through its full lifecycle -
Draft, Submitted, Accepted, Fulfilled, Received - with status tabs and search.
Buyers receive stock by mapping delivered lines to products (stock and cost
update together); sellers accept (auto-creating the buyer's invoice), reject
with a reason, or fulfill (auto-deducting stock). You can also list your own
products for supply on the B2B marketplace and list them on your public shop.

### 6.6 Dashboard & Reports

The dashboard is the "know your numbers" screen: five KPI cards (Net Sales
Today, Today's Sales, Products Sold, Today's Expenses, Total Customers), a
7-day sales trend, low-stock alerts, a live feed of today's receipts, an
expenses-by-category chart, and a VAT position. Branches can be compared, and
professional reports generated and downloaded as PDF, Excel, or CSV: Business
Summary (P&L), Daily Sales, Product Performance, Shift Reconciliation,
Expenses, VAT Summary, Inventory, Payment Breakdown, and Full Branch
Performance - with presets and filters by sales person or shift.

### 6.7 Customers

Build a customer profile (name, phone, email) in seconds; every sale can
attach to that person automatically. The **Customers Overview** segments the
base into Active, At risk, Lapsed, and Never purchased, with repeat rate,
revenue, and top spenders. Per-customer purchase history is one click away,
and customers can be attached or created mid-sale at checkout - all offline.

### 6.8 Expenses, Budgets & Income

Log any expense in seconds, organize it under categories that can each carry a
budget (weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly), attach a receipt photo or link
(every shilling provable), and set up personal budgets with shopping lists
that convert to expenses. Non-sales income (salary, freelance, rent) can be
recorded, and an **Income & Expenses overview** shows the full picture. Export
everything to CSV or Excel for the accountant - offline-first throughout.

### 6.9 Accounting

Double-entry books without an accountant at the till. Every sale, refund,
expense, invoice, and payment writes a balanced journal entry automatically.
Manual entries can be posted (unbalanced ones won't save), posted entries can
be reversed cleanly, and finished months can be locked. Five print-ready
financial statements are available for any period - Trial Balance, P&L
(income statement), Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and Equity - plus financial
ratios with recommendations, fixed-asset depreciation, inventory-to-ledger
reconciliation, and import/export of accounts and entries.

### 6.10 Financial Forecasting

See where money stands and where it is heading: cash available, monthly burn,
and cash runway (how many months you can keep paying everyone). Build a
zero-based year budget with line justification and approval, roll forecast
snapshots, and run **what-if scenarios** (a new hire, extra spend, or sales
growth) against real cash math before committing. Forecast KPIs cover sales
pulse, CAC, LTV, churn, and SaaS-style MRR/ARR proxies for recurring products.

### 6.11 Documents Vault

A searchable file vault: named cabinets (Receipts, Contracts, Tax), file
uploads and web links, nested folders (including importing a whole local
folder), and search by name or tag. Access is controlled per file/folder with
Viewer / Contributor / Manager roles. Files preview and even edit inline
(text, CSV as a table), email or download as zip, link to customers and
projects, and every change is tracked in a per-cabinet activity feed.

### 6.12 E-commerce Storefront

Turn the physical shop into a public online store: enable the shop, claim an
@username, list products, and share one link (and a printable QR code).
Customers browse the **Discover** feed (search/filter by category, city,
currency, price, rating), browse a shop with live stock availability, add to a
multi-cart bag (one per shop), place orders, track them live, and get receipts
or invoices. Wishlists, favorites, and ratings are built in, and online orders
flow straight into the business's order management.

### 6.13 Sales Pipeline (CRM)

A visual sales board to track every opportunity: a ready-made pipeline (New,
Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Closed won/lost) that can be
reshaped to how the business actually sells. Leads carry value, due dates, and
priority; cards drag through stages; follow-up reminders keep deals warm; a
won lead converts to a customer in one click; and insights show pipeline
value, win rates, and converted customers. Teams collaborate on the board with
conversations, announcements, polls, automations, goals, board resources, a
wall of fame, and a public booking page that turns bookings into leads.

### 6.14 Estimates, Templates & Projects

Quote, deliver, and get paid in one flow. Build professional estimates with
line items priced from cost + markup and a live gross-profit margin, send or
email them as PDFs, and approve them - converting to an invoice and/or a
project in one step. Projects track budget vs actuals with early cost-overrun
alerts, tasks, timesheets, and cost allocations, and each project gets its own
kanban board (To Do, In Progress, Review, Done) born automatically from the
estimate. Reusable estimate templates, lead-to-estimate handoff, personal
boards, client board-only views (costs hidden), and team roles are included.

### 6.15 HR & Payroll

Bring the whole team into one place: employees with departments and positions,
app logins, attendance via clock in/out, leave types and approvals, salary
structures, and full payroll runs with Uganda statutory taxes (**PAYE and
NSSF**) - calculate, approve, post to accounting, settle, and remit. Payroll
affordability and hire modeling show the cash impact of every payday and new
hire. Also covered: onboarding checklists, performance scoring, company asset
tracking, statutory schedules, and staff transfers between branches.

### 6.16 Settings & Workspaces

The control room: business profile and logo, location/currency, tax details
(TIN, VAT registration, default rate, prices-include-tax), bank and mobile
money payment details printed on invoices, branded receipts, VAT return
preparation with EFRIS status, and full data export (JSON/CSV/Excel - "no
lock-in"). Team management covers staff invites, custom roles (cashier,
salesperson, manager) with per-module access, branches, and toggling
workspaces on/off (e.g. Full Projects & Estimates, Full HR & Payroll).

### 6.17 Quick Notes

Built-in notes for the whole team: capture reminders, customer preferences,
and ideas in seconds with title, body, color, and tag; search or filter by
tag; pin important notes; share notes with the organization (business
accounts); and restyle the board. Notes save instantly even offline.

### 6.18 Notifications & Web Push

One inbox for team and pipeline updates with unread badges, mark-all-read, and
delete controls. Beyond the inbox, **web push** delivers desktop notifications
(new orders, sales, account updates) even when the app is closed, and a sound
chime can alert on new orders - with an urgent chime for big orders above a
configurable threshold.

### 6.19 Referrals, Credits & Sales Reps

The referral program turns happy customers into a marketing channel. Any
business generates a personal referral code and shares it as a copyable code,
a signup link, or a downloadable QR code. When a new business signs up with
the code **and subscribes**, the referrer earns a reward based on the plan
actually paid. New signups can apply a friend's code right after account
creation for a discount on their subscription. The **Referral Dashboard** shows
referrals, earned, paid, and balance, each referred business with its status
(pending, active, rewarded), and payout history. Rewards are paid via Mobile
Money or Bank Transfer to any bank worldwide. Businesses enrolled as **sales
reps** see a Sales Rep badge with their commission rate, and commissions are
combined with referral rewards in one earnings view. Rewards are based on
what's actually paid, with no limit to how many businesses you can refer.

### 6.20 Offline-First & Sync

Custosell keeps working when the internet drops. An amber banner confirms your
work is saved on the device, checkout keeps working, and everything **syncs
automatically** when you reconnect - in dependency order, with a visible
progress banner, and retry on failure. Working offline: sales and payments,
products, customers, expenses, shifts, orders and refunds, stock adjustments,
quick notes, account registration, and feedback. Offline-created records get
remapped to server IDs, and the sync queue is durable (IndexedDB plus backup)
so nothing is lost. A "sync pending" badge appears on items not yet uploaded.

### 6.21 The Guide (Training & Support)

Custosell ships with a built-in help center: a **Tutorials library** of short
video guides searchable and filterable by category, an **FAQ library** grouped
by topic with live search, and **feedback** (general or feature request) that
is offline-first - messages save on the device and send when reconnected, with
tracked status (Submitted, Acknowledged, In progress, Resolved, Closed) and
team replies. **Contact & Help** shows the support email and phone lines with
one-tap copy for Call or WhatsApp.

## 9. The Referral & Sales-Rep Program (How You Earn)

Custosell rewards referrals and pays sales reps - and you can be both.

**Referral rewards:**

- Every business gets one personal referral code (copyable, as a signup link,
  or a QR code for print/offline).
- When a new business signs up with your code **and subscribes**, you earn a
  reward based on the plan they actually pay.
- **One referral code per referred business**; **no limit** on how many
  businesses you can refer; rewards are on what's actually paid.
- The Referral Dashboard tracks Referrals, Earned, Paid, and Balance, with a
  list of each referred business and its status (pending, active, rewarded)
  plus payout history.

**Sales-rep commissions:**

- Businesses enrolled as sales reps see a **Sales Rep badge** showing their
  commission rate (rate and type) on the Referral Dashboard.
- Referral rewards and commissions are combined into one earnings view, and
  payouts appear in the same history.
- Set payout details once (Mobile Money or Bank Transfer to any bank
  worldwide) and future payouts just work.

**In the field, remember:** the discount a new business applies is on their
subscription; your reward/commission is a percentage of what is actually
paid - never on a discounted figure's sticker price.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions (Objections)

**Q: Is Custosell really free to try?**
A: Yes - every plan includes a 30-day trial. A one-time onboarding/setup fee
activates the account, then the trial period begins before your first billing
cycle.

**Q: What happens if the internet goes down?**
A: The POS keeps working. Sales, payments, products, customers, expenses,
shifts, orders, refunds, stock adjustments, notes, and even registration all
work offline. Everything syncs automatically when the connection returns -
with a progress banner and retry, so nothing is lost.

**Q: Is my data safe offline?**
A: Yes. Offline data is stored locally on the device with the same encryption
standards and isolated to the Custosell app; data syncs securely when
connectivity is restored.

**Q: Can a business switch plans later?**
A: Yes - upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades apply immediately with
proration (you only pay the difference); downgrades apply at the end of the
billing period so no paid days are lost.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money), bank transfers, and PesaPal with
your phone; card payments coming soon. In UGX, KES, and TZS via PesaPal,
otherwise USD.

**Q: Is there a discount for paying yearly?**
A: Yes - yearly billing reduces the effective monthly rate.

**Q: Does Custosell handle taxes and payroll for Uganda?**
A: Yes. VAT setup and VAT return preparation are built in, and HR & Payroll
handles PAYE and NSSF (employer + employee) with statutory schedules for
filing.

**Q: Can I run more than one branch or business?**
A: Yes - the Professional plan supports up to 3 businesses and 3 locations,
Enterprise up to 5 businesses and 5 locations with unlimited staff/products,
and branch comparison, stock transfers, and cross-branch reports are built in.

**Q: Can customers buy from me online without me building a website?**
A: Yes. Turn on the public shop, claim an @username, list products, and share
one link (and a QR code). Customers browse, order, and track - and stock stays
tied to your inventory automatically.

**Q: I already use spreadsheets. Why switch?**
A: Spreadsheets don't update themselves. With Custosell, one sale updates
stock, books, and reports together; the drawer reconciles at shift close; and
your accountant gets clean, downloadable reports - no manual rekeying.

**Q: What support will the business get?**
A: A built-in Tutorials library and FAQs, offline-first feedback with tracked
replies, and direct support by email or phone (Monday - Friday, 8 AM - 6 PM
EAT) at support@custosell.com / +256 756 697 871.

**Q: Can I run a personal (non-business) account?**
A: Yes - the Personal plan ($9.99/month) covers pipeline, estimates, expenses,
accounting, and documents for personal productivity.

**Q: Does Custosell lock me in?**
A: No. Owners can export their data anytime (JSON, CSV, or Excel) from
Settings - "no lock-in."
